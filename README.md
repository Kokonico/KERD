# KERDL
> Kinetic Energy Redirection Device Launcher

Todos:
	* Improve air movement
	* Levels & Models
	* The rest of the game
	
Levels:
	1 (Intro). Basic jump off existing KERD. teaches that KERDs are just trampolines
	2 (Angles). Introduces angled KERDs.
	3 (Thinking with KURDL). Introduces the KURDL. "Reach around behind your back". Place KURD and bounce to exit.
	4 (Quick KURDLing) Repeat of level 2, but you must place kurdles.
	5 (Zig-Zag). Two vertical walls, must KURD the sides to zig-bounce across.
	6 (Supergravity). long vertical chamber, must use KURDs upside-down to go down fast. Room will be incinerated and falling takes too long before incineration
	7 (What goes up...) chamber 18 from portal 1, but with KURDS. Several platforms, each a bit taller than the last. Use KURDS to get to each one.
	8 (...Must come down). Mostly cutscene, elevator you're in creaks to a halt, lights stop. creaks. floor breaks from under you, you start falling down shaft. as you're falling, you see a platform in the distance. you fire the KURDL. *shine*, you rapidly approach and hit the KURDL, 3 seconds of hitting it, then you're launched back up the shaft at supersonic speed, you fly through the elevator (convinient hole in roof already there.), then you go through the broken ceiling of the building and see the wonderful city below (image skybox, not physical.). cuts to black screen with credits.
